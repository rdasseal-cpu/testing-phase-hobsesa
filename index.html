Birth of HOBSESA & Our Journey (Estd. 1996)

The Hooghly Branch (Govt) School Ex-Students' Association was officially established in 1996 at Chawk Bazar, Hooghly, West Bengal to strengthen unity and fraternity among former and present students, registered under the Registrar of Societies, Government of West Bengal with Registration No. S/93009.

Birth of HOBSESA

On a pleasant evening in the third week of September 1996, at the invitation of Dr. Ashok Banerjee, a few alumni of the 1968 academic year gathered at the Punjab Club, Ballygunge and decided to organize a reunion ceremony at school with the alumni of the two academic years, 1967 and 1968.

Consequently, on October 26, 1996, a meeting was held at the school in the presence of 80 alumni from these two years. In that grand meeting, it was decided that a registered Association would be formed, not just with the alumni of these two years, but with the alumni of all academic sessions of the school.

Based on that meeting's decision, the Hooghly Branch (Govt.) School Ex-Students' Association (HOBSESA) was formed. Therefore, October 26th is considered the foundation day of HOBSESA.

In the first meeting of the Executive Committee of HOBSESA, it was decided that apart from the annual reunion festival, HOBSESA would also perform some social welfare activities to the best of its ability. It was decided that the reunion festival would be held every year on the first Saturday and Sunday of February. According to that decision, the first reunion festival of HOBSESA was held on the 2nd and 3rd February, 1997.

Due to current changed examination calendar, the reunion festival day of the Association has been brought forward to January instead of February starting from the year 2024.

Entering our landmark 30th year in 2026, the association continues to expand its purpose—organizing educational fairs, health camps, inter-school sports tournaments, and providing vital academic scholarships to needy and deserving students.

Vision

To build a strong lifelong bond among alumni and current students while fostering educational excellence, social responsibility, cultural heritage, and holistic development.

Mission

  • Strengthen unity and fraternity among ex- and present students.
  • Promote physical, mental, educational, and cultural development.
  • Encourage knowledge creation, exchange of ideas, and academic excellence.
  • Support students through educational and social initiatives.
"It gives me immense pleasure and a deep sense of pride to welcome you all to the official launch of the website of the Hooghly Branch Government School Ex-Students Association.

This platform is not merely a digital presence; it is a living bridge connecting generations of students who have walked through the corridors of our beloved institution. Our school has not only imparted education but has shaped values, character, and a lifelong sense of belonging.

Our Association, founded in 1996, has now proudly stepped into its 30th year—a significant milestone that reflects decades of unity, shared memories, and collective commitment. Beyond fellowship, we consistently engage in blood donation camps, health check-ups, thalassemia awareness, health quizzes, and awarding scholarships to needy and deserving students.

I invite all alumni to actively participate, enrich this platform, and help us build a stronger, more connected community."
Dr. Partha Tripathi President, Hooghly Branch Government School Ex-Students Association

Major Initiatives & Social Activities

Our Association conducts a rich calendar of annual programs designed for academic enhancement, community healthcare, sports development, and social welfare:

🧠

Inter-school Quiz Competition

Fostering academic curiosity, awareness, and healthy competition among students across schools in Hooghly.

🔬

Science Exhibition

Providing a creative platform for young innovators to demonstrate science projects and technological ideas.

🩺

Free Health Camp & Thalassemia Awareness

Conducting comprehensive health checkups, medical advice, and spreading critical thalassemia awareness.

🎓

Support for Higher Education

Guidance and financial support for meritorious students striving to pursue higher academic programs.

🏅

Anil Kumar Deb Memorial Scholarship

Awarding scholarships to deserving and financially needy students to empower their educational journey.

🎵

Inter-school Music Competition

Promoting cultural heritage, vocal talents, and artistic expression among school students.

💻

Computer Education Mela

Exposing students to digital literacy, computer skills, software tools, and emerging technology careers.

📜

Prize Distribution to Successful Students

Recognizing and rewarding academic excellence and outstanding achievements in board examinations.

🧥

Blanket Distribution Camp

Winter humanitarian drive providing warm blankets and essentials to vulnerable individuals.

🎨

Sit and Draw Competition

Annual art and drawing contest encouraging creative imagination among young school children.

📖

Publication of Annual Souvenir

Publishing an annual souvenir containing literary contributions, reports, and historic photos.

🎤

Seminars & Workshops

Organizing educational seminars, carrier guidance workshops, and interactive alumni lectures.

🩸

Blood Donation Camp

Organizing life-saving blood donation drives with voluntary participation from members and citizens.

⚽

Football Coaching Camp & Invitational Tournament

Running football coaching camps & organizing the prestigious Surajit Sengupta & Ashwini Barat Inter-School Invitational Football Tournament.
